# Photos from under the surface: shafts of light, the surface as a mirror, waves from below

From Wikimedia Commons, 20 November 2026, each scaled to about 1280 pixels across (the kelp photo is its original,
960 pixels). None seems to use a strobe or flash. The files by Jeremy Bishop and Tim Marshall came to Commons from
Unsplash and are toned the way surf photographs usually are: use them for shapes and light, not measured colour.
