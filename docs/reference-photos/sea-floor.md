# The sea floor in and around coral reefs and atolls

From Wikimedia Commons, 20 November 2026, for repainting the ground under water: lagoon sand, reef flat, rubble,
hard reef floor. Each is the copy Commons serves for 1600 pixels across, which is now 1920 pixels (the nearest
standard size), or the original where that is smaller; four are PNG because they were published so. None seems
to use a flash unless the entry says so. Sizes are judged from the animals, divers and corals in the frame and
are rough. Commons search turned up few wide, natural-light pictures of plain floor; many here are pictures of
an animal on the floor, kept for the floor round it.

Two are not under water (the Guam reef flats at low tide) and two look down from above the water; they are
there for the floor's shapes and patterns, not for its colour under water.
