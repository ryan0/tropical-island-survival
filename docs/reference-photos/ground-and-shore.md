# Photos of the ground and shore of coral atolls

From Wikimedia Commons, 19 November 2026. Each is Commons' scaled copy, asked for at 1600 pixels across; Commons now
serves its nearest standard size, so every file is 1920 pixels across. Not re-encoded.

All are of atoll islets except two marked below (Heron Island is a coral cay on the Great Barrier Reef; Eriyadu is a
Maldives atoll island, but the picture is of the swash, not the ground). The Midway photos are of an atoll, but its
islets carry ironwood and shrubs rather than coconut palms.
